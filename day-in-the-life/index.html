A Day in the Life
of the Nasdaq-100®

L O A D I N G . . .

The Nasdaq-100 spans industries from tech to consumer goods, industrials, and beyond. Click to explore how these innovative companies are embedded in our everyday lives.

Read Story

Amy Richards ushers in her day with her Google (GOOGL) Home reading her the weather report.

Before work she throws on her go-to Lululemon (LULU) athletic wear and heads out for a walk while streaming SiriusXM Radio (SIRI) on her T-Mobile (TMUS) 5G network.

Amy stops by Starbucks (SBUX) for a coffee.

She takes a moment to check the delivery date of a chair she ordered from Amazon (AMZN) using her Apple (AAPL) iPhone.

Amy meets her client downtown for a project check-in. Her partners who are traveling for business join them via Zoom (ZM).

They finalize the production agreement, using DocuSign (DOCU) for signatures.

Amy adds some final touches using InDesign, an Adobe (ADBE) offering, to a project she’s been working on. She snacks on SunChips, a PepsiCo (PEP) product, while she wraps up her work.

Amy leaves the office early to visit the dentist for a consult about her Invisalign— a product of Align Technologies (ALGN).

Before heading home, Amy swings by Walgreens (WBA), Costco (COST), and Ross Stores (ROST) to pick up some essentials.

Amy plans an upcoming trip, snagging a deal on Booking.com (BKNG).

and an Airbnb (ABNB) just a few miles from the beach.

Amy heads back out to catch up with an old friend at the Marriott Marquis, a chain owned by Marriott International (MAR).

Once she gets home, she checks a message from an old friend on Facebook, the Meta (META)-owned social platform.

Before bed, she plays one of her Electronic Arts (EA) games.

Amy steps outside for some fresh air before bed, looking out in amazement at the smart city streets , connected by Qualcomm (QCOM).
