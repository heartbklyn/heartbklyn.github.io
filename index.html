The Venue

Rose Hill Farm is a historic pick-your-own fruit orchard located in Red Hook, New York.

Rose Hill grows cherries, blueberries, plums, apricots, peaches, and apples, which they also use to make natural wines and ciders.

Robbie and Olivia have spent countless weekends picking fruit at the farm, and since 2021, Olivia has taught a weekly yoga class under the orchard's beautiful and exceptionally wise willow tree.

This farm is one of Olivia and Robbie's favorite places and the perfect place to celebrate their wedding.
